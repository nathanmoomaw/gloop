# ETHOS — GLOOP

The room talks back to itself.

GLOOP doesn't play sound at you — it takes the sound already happening around it and hands it back, chopped into grains, smeared through feedback, never quite the same twice. No presets to browse, no track to finish. Just a live loop between what the space makes and what GLOOP returns.

Grains, not samples. Nothing here is a recording of a bell or a drop of water pretending to be real — it's the mic input itself, granulated and refracted. The instrument is whatever room you're in.

The visualization isn't decoration. Chladni plates make sound visible by letting it arrange sand into the patterns of its own standing waves. GLOOP's grain field does the same thing with color — the pattern on screen is a direct readout of what the ears would notice if they looked.

This is a mirror with a delay in it, not a synthesizer.
